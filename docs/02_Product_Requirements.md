# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name
KudiFlow

### Tagline
Flow Smarter. Grow Wealthier.

### Product Description
KudiFlow is a personal finance management application designed to help individuals track their income and expenses, manage budgets, build savings habits, and gain meaningful insights into their financial health through a simple and intuitive experience.

### Purpose
The purpose of KudiFlow is to empower users to make informed financial decisions by providing tools that simplify money management and encourage positive financial habits.

### Target Audience
- Students
- Young professionals
- Salaried workers
- Freelancers
- Small business owners

## 2. Problem Statement

### Background

Managing personal finances is a challenge for many individuals because they lack simple tools that help them understand their spending patterns, control expenses, and build consistent saving habits.

Many people receive income regularly but struggle to answer basic questions such as:

- Where did my money go this month?
- How much am I spending on unnecessary expenses?
- Am I saving enough?
- Can I afford a major purchase?

Existing financial tools are often complex, designed for specific markets, or do not provide a personalized experience suitable for everyday users.

### Problem KudiFlow Solves

KudiFlow addresses these challenges by providing a simple financial companion that allows users to:

- Track income and expenses easily
- Create and manage budgets
- Set and monitor savings goals
- Understand spending habits through reports and insights
- Make better financial decisions

### Goal

The goal of KudiFlow is to make personal finance management simple, accessible, and practical for everyday users.
